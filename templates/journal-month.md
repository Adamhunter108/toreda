# Trading journal — YYYY MONTH

A plain-text record of trades and reasoning. One entry per action.

**Write the reasoning before the outcome is known.** A journal written after the
fact records what happened, not what you believed — which means it cannot tell
you the difference between a good decision and a lucky one. The "wrong if" line
is what makes a later review possible.

Journal decisions with *no* trade too: a rule you changed, a signal you
deliberately ignored, a thesis you abandoned. Those are the entries that pay off.
